What shall we explore?

Addition totals and subtraction starting numbers stay within this range. Multiplication factors and division answers go up to this number (so a range of 10 includes 10 × 10 = 100 and 100 ÷ 10 = 10).

Focus numbers

Focus means the number you add, take away, multiply by, or divide by. Select 2 and 4 to practise just those numbers. All numbers also includes zero where it is valid.

Problem order

Sequential works through each operation, then each focus number, from smallest to largest. Shuffle mixes that same set without repeats until the set is complete.
